The Android game

The game stores your chosen guest name, language, scores, career and learning progress, and virtual purchases on your device so you can resume play. It does not require a user account or send your game profile to a game server. Clear the app’s data to remove the local profile. Any device-level backup is governed by your system settings.

If you select a CSV file for a replay, the game processes it on the device for that round. When you choose to open an external link, your browser contacts the destination.

This website

The site serves its images and gameplay video directly, without analytics, ad trackers or third-party video embeds. GitHub Pages hosts it. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details on GitHub’s handling of this data.

Email and your rights

When you email support, I use the address and information you provide to answer your inquiry. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in responding to requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), or Article 6(1)(b) if the request is necessary to handle a contract. I retain correspondence as needed to resolve the inquiry or meet legal retention duties (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

You can contact me about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable. You can also complain to the Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde or another competent supervisory authority.
